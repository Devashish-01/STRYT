My STRYT, My People.

Welcome to STRYT — your street, your people, your neighborhood marketplace.

STRYT transforms how you discover, connect, and transact with local shops, skilled service providers, and neighbors in your immediate vicinity. From morning chai and fresh produce to trusted salon bookings and home repairs, STRYT brings the warmth and vitality of Indian street commerce right to your smartphone.

🌟 WHY YOU'LL LOVE STRYT:

📍 HYPERLOCAL DISCOVERY & REAL-TIME MAP
• Discover nearby stores, food stalls, home-based businesses, and artisans within walking distance.
• Real-time availability: See who is open right now, view live product catalogs, and check service menus before you step out.
• Smart neighborhood filtering: Search by groceries, street food, salon, repair, fitness, and more.

🤝 STREET REQUESTS & LIVE BARGAINING
• Need something specific? Post a street request to nearby shops in seconds.
• Receive real-time counter-offers and proposals directly from local vendors.
• Negotiate fair prices with live offers — bringing authentic street bargaining into the digital era without middlemen cuts.

✂️ INSTANT APPOINTMENT BOOKINGS
• Book haircuts, salon treatments, repair consultations, and wellness sessions with zero waiting.
• Interactive slot picker: Choose your preferred day and time with real-time slot verification.
• Instant booking confirmations, reminders, and directions to the vendor's doorstep.

⚡ 100% DIRECT, ZERO-COMMISSION UPI
• Pay vendors directly with your preferred UPI app — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any banking app.
• STRYT takes 0% commission on peer-to-peer transactions. 100% of your money goes straight to the hardworking local merchant.
• On-device payment QR verification and offline receipts ensure transparent, immediate settlement.

🛡️ "MY PEOPLE" LIVE LOCATION SHARING
• Choose to share your live location with people you trust — you start it, and only you.
• Sharing keeps running while your phone is locked, so they can follow your journey.
• Stop any time with one tap, and it ends on its own after 8 hours.

🛵 HOME DELIVERY FROM LOCAL SHOPS
• Order from shops that deliver and follow your order from "packing" to "on the way", with a live estimate of when it arrives.
• A hand-over code keeps your order safe: only the person who has the code receives it.
• Shops get a simple console to accept, pack and assign orders; delivery partners get one screen that always shows the next step.
• A delivery partner's location is used only while they are delivering. Customers see progress and an arrival time — never a location.

📢 STREET ALERTS & COMMUNITY UPDATES
• Stay informed with neighborhood broadcasts, flash discounts, street events, and utility alerts.
• Safe, verified community interactions designed to strengthen local neighborhood bonds.

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST:
• No annoying advertisements or intrusive ad trackers.
• Your location is used only for nearby discovery, user-initiated live sharing, and — for delivery partners — tracking a delivery while it is under way. We never sell your personal data or track your movement history.
• End-to-end encrypted transit (HTTPS/TLS) across all communications.
• Easily manage your data or request account deletion at any time directly in-app or via our transparent web portal.

Download STRYT today and rediscover the pulse of your street!
