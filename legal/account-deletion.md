# STRYT — Delete Your Account

**Last Updated:** 4 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your STRYT account and what happens to your
data when you do. It forms part of the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) and
the [Data Retention Policy](data-retention-policy.md).

You do **not** need to install the app, contact support, or wait for an
administrator. Deletion is self-service.

---

## 1. How to request deletion

**In the app (or on the web at [stryt.in](https://stryt.in)):**

1. Open **Account** (bottom navigation).
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Optionally tell us why, then confirm.

You can go straight there with
**[stryt.in/settings/data?action=delete](https://stryt.in/settings/data?action=delete)**
— you will be asked to sign in first, because we only accept a deletion request
from the account holder.

**If you cannot sign in** — you lost access to your Google account, or the
account is already locked — email **contact@stryt.in** from any address you can
still access, and include enough detail for us to identify the account (the
phone number or display name on it). We will verify ownership before acting, and
respond within the timelines in the
[Grievance Redressal Policy](grievance-redressal-policy.md).

---

## 2. The 30-day grace period

Once you confirm, your account is **scheduled for deletion in 30 days**.

- It is immediately hidden from other users — your profile, listings, and posts
  stop being visible.
- You can **cancel in one tap** by signing in again during those 30 days.
- After 30 days, deletion completes automatically and is **not reversible**.

---

## 3. What deletion removes

When deletion completes, STRYT:

- **anonymises your user record** — your name is replaced with "Deleted User",
  your phone number is zeroed, your avatar is cleared, and the account is marked
  disabled;
- **deletes your uploaded files** — avatars, stories, request photos, business,
  catalogue and portfolio photos, and **KYC / verification documents**;
- **deletes the Business and Provider profiles you own**, together with their
  catalogue items, offers, portfolio items, packages and stories;
- **deletes your sign-in identity**, so the account can no longer be used to log
  in; and
- **records the deletion** in our audit log.

---

## 4. What may remain, and why

Some limited data survives deletion. This is normal and is explained in full in
[Data Retention Policy §3.4](data-retention-policy.md):

- **Messages other people already received** from you, and anything they saved
  or copied, are outside our unilateral control.
- **Records tied to someone else's rights** — a completed transaction, a review
  you left about a seller, or an open dispute — may be kept in anonymised or
  minimal form for that party's records, for fraud prevention, and for legal
  defence.
- **Audit and compliance logs** are retained for security and accountability.
- **Anonymised or aggregate data** that no longer identifies you may be kept.
- **Encrypted backups** may hold copies briefly until they cycle out.

---

## 5. Before you can delete

Deletion cannot complete while you have:

- an **active Deal or Agreement** that is not completed, cancelled, or disputed;
  or
- a payment record in a **"held"** status in the Deal tracker.

Resolve those first, then retry. (These are status checks inside STRYT — STRYT
never holds your money. See [Terms & Conditions](terms-and-conditions.md) §13.)

---

## 6. Deleting only a Business or Provider profile

You can delete a Business or Provider profile **without** closing your personal
account: open the profile you manage → **Settings** → **Delete**. The same
active-deal guardrails apply. Your customer account continues to work.

---

## 7. Other options

If you want to step away without deleting, you can instead:

- turn off notifications (**Account → Notifications**),
- stop any live location share (**My People → Stop sharing**), or
- **download a copy of your data** first (**Account → Download my data**).
