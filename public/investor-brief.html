01 / THE CORE THESIS

A credible, asset-light operating platform

STRYT organizes neighborhood commerce through software infrastructure—not by leasing expensive dark stores, taking inventory risk, or subsidizing hyper-burn logistics.

The Merchant Pain

Monopolistic aggregators impose 22%–30% take-rate commissions, mask buyer identities, and extort ad spend within the seller's own 2-km radius.

The Product Engine

A unified PWA enabling instant storefronts, reverse demand matching ("Ask Street"), time-slot queues, and autonomous local dispatch.

The Economic Moat

0% core transaction commission attracts merchants virally; monetization is driven by predictable SaaS, DaaS micro-dispatch, and hyper-targeted ads.

“The multi-billion dollar opportunity is not to own the neighborhood’s inventory. It is to become the indispensable software layer that makes the neighborhood digitally competitive.”
Investment Readiness: STRYT is presented here as an operational software hypothesis with verified market macro tailwinds. Active paying cohorts and GMV traction are subject to local cluster pilot audits.
02 / MACRO CATALYSTS

Why the aggregator monopoly is vulnerable today

Three macroeconomic forces converge in India to create an opening for an open, merchant-first operating platform.

1
Traditional Retail Dominates Physical Commerce. Verified Market Data

IBEF measures Indian retail at $1.094 Trillion in 2025. Over 80% of purchases occur within 2 km of the buyer's home at neighborhood Kiranas and independent establishments. [1]

2
Ubiquitous Zero-MDR Payment Rails. NPCI Official

UPI processed 25.47 billion transactions in October 2026 alone (~₹31.53 Lakh Cr). Consumers and merchants are fully habituated to instant mobile settlements, bypassing credit card gateway fees. [2]

3
The Dark-Store Unit Economics Wall. Analyst Estimate

Quick-commerce players face mounting urban lease costs, perishable inventory shrinkage (dairy, fruit), and rider attrition. Neighborhood stores already hold that inventory 300 meters away at zero platform capex. [4]

4
Open Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI). PIB Official

ONDC has scaled to 500,000+ sellers across 1,000+ cities. The regulatory and consumer sentiment is decisively shifting toward open, anti-monopoly merchant enablement. [3]

Market Sizing Discipline: Institutional investors should distinguish India's total $1.15T retail GMV from STRYT's addressable software market. STRYT's addressable market is defined by software subscriptions, dispatch micro-fees, and promotional revenues.
03 / PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

4-in-1 Unified Persona Engine

Unlike fragmented platforms that silo apps into food, grocery, salons, or courier services, STRYT enables one universal account to switch seamlessly between four roles in any neighborhood.

🛍️

Customer

Post reverse requests ("Ask Street"), compare local bids, book time slots, and track delivery on a live map.

🏪

Store Merchant

Publish instant catalogs, receive localized demand, manage virtual queues, and dispatch in-house or pooled riders.

⚡

Service Provider

Home technicians, salons, and repair pros quote directly on community leads with mutual milestone escrow.

🛵

Delivery Rider

Local gig partners or store runners accept batch runs, turn-by-turn routing, and OTP doorstep handoffs.

Single Unified PWA · Zero App-Store Friction · Cross-Network Liquidity

Reverse Demand Marketplace ("Ask Your Street")

Customers post what they need ("5L cold-pressed oil by 5 PM"). Surrounding verified merchants compete on pricing and availability. Solves the cold-start problem without heavy advertising.

Direct Peer-to-Merchant (P2M) UPI

Payment flows straight to the merchant's UPI VPA. Eliminates 7-day aggregator settlement holds and credit risk, building immediate merchant trust.

Autonomous Dispatch Coordination

Merchants choose self-delivery or tap nearby pooled riders via a ₹5–₹10 micro-coordination fee. Real-time GPS tracking and 6-digit OTP verification ensure handoff security.

Virtual Walk-in Queues & Appointments

Eliminates street crowding for clinics, salons, and food spots with live token countdowns and automated SMS/push notifications.
